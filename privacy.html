Privacy Notice: Weekly Placement Report

Applies to the Weekly Placement Report form and the supervisor review page at eic-college.github.io/placement.

What we collect

  • Students: name and college email (to confirm you are on your class list), the shift times you report, your weekly summary of activities, and your typed signature.
  • Preceptors/supervisors: your name, the email address the college has on file, your review decision, comments and typed signature.

How it is used

Only to record and verify placement hours: reports are sent to the student's preceptor/supervisor for verification, and verified records (including a PDF) are kept by the college for official record-keeping. We do not sell or share this information for any other purpose.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Records are stored in the college's Google account (Google Sheets and Drive). They are available to authorized EICC placement staff, and each report is shown to the student's own preceptor/supervisor. Emails about reports are sent from placementseicc@gmail.com.

Questions or corrections

Contact the EICC placement office at placement@eiccollege.ca or 613-900-4473. Excellence Imperial Career College, 2442 St Joseph Boulevard, Unit 102, Ottawa, ON K1C 1G1.
